Hi everyone

Late newsletter this month!

I was waiting for my newest release to be available for preorder on bandcamp so I could share the news here.

Dunghutti Interchange and Green Line are available to stream free during pre-order with the full mixtape released on next months Bandcamp Friday (3/7/2026)

I wrote a silly announcement on bandcamp for my followers that I kind of regret, saying this mixtape was a golden shower of piss-takes, songs with a general apathy towards songwriting and being a musician.

In all honesty though, they're more than that! I enjoyed making them and playing them live throughout last year. So please, check out the mixtape when you get a chance.

Let me know where you end up listening, whether its the digi dub deodorant track while you're getting ready in the morning or my Doctor Karl diss track in your car. Wherever it is I'd love to know!

Hi everyone

Earlier this year I removed all Club Moss music from streaming platforms.

I was a bit lazy in taking so long to do this, but am happy with the decision to not be a part of that system.

I'm not interested in rehashing the reasons here so anyone interested can visit these articles & podcasts articulating some of the issues with streaming services. All Club Moss releases and other music I've self-released are still available to listen to, hosted on Bandcamp, where I will be releasing music for the first time since my release with Midheaven in 2024.

Next months release is a collection of demos from 2025 that people enjoyed from shows that need to be archived so I can move on and make new music

You can listen and download Green Line and untitled song for the yaris before they're on bandcamp by clicking the music icons.

P.S.

I've been thinking about photography a lot recently and going through photobooks after work at Fisher Library. A PDF of scans is on the newsletter desktop for you to see of photos I was looking at today.
